How to read this

% met+ is the share of tested students at or above “standard met”. Distance from standard is the average scale score minus the grade-level cut score for “standard met” (positive is above standard); it is calculated here from the published mean scale score, and matches the Dashboard's method for the all-grades measure only approximately. Combined 3–5 and group averages are weighted by students tested. Peer groups are the closest elementary schools to McKinley on 2024–25 enrollment, low-income share, English learner share and Hispanic/Latino share; the multilingual-program group is the 20 closest matches among schools on the state's multilingual-program list, plus any other selected school on that list. The median and the three bands (Not met, Nearly met plus Met, Exceeded) are built from the share of students in each of the state's four performance levels; they are not equal quartiles and the median is an estimate. The English learner test (ELPAC) covers only students identified as English learners, so its “All Students” row means all English learners; its four levels run from beginning to well developed. Science is tested in grade 5 only. There was no statewide testing in 2019–20. Beginning in 2025–26 the state renamed the four performance levels (Advanced, Proficient, Developing, Minimal); the data maps them to the earlier Exceeded, Met, Nearly Met, Not Met.
