# Shellfie: grade placement and school-content guidance

This is the shared starting point for contributors and agents reviewing, writing, translating or categorizing Shellfie books. Follow the user's instructions and a school's applicable policies. These are Shellfie editorial decisions informed by the standards below, not an official state rating system, a legal determination, or a claim that every book is approved for every California school.

- [Grade placement and content guidance](grade-placement.md)
- [Existing book inventory](book-inventory.csv): current assigned grades, not independently verified reading levels or suitability approvals.
- [Revision log](../books/REVISION_LOG.md): what was changed, why, and by whom or by which process.
- [Story and quiz test cases](../tests/content_suitability.md) and [review record](../tests/content_review_2026-11-07.md).

Generate a fresh inventory from the repository root with `python3 shellfie/standards/update_inventory.py`. Existing grades are starting points, not automatic validation. When adding or reassigning a book, record its source and copyright/permission basis, edition and language, original and proposed grades, qualitative and quantitative evidence, task demands, suitability review, reviewer, and date. An adaptation's edition and quiz can differ in complexity from the original. Mark unreviewed items as unreviewed. Do not infer reading ability from a child's grade or treat a single readability score as a placement decision.
