## Proposed edits

### P-001 — First grade: St. Valentine's Day

`en-1-365-bedtime`, chapter 45, “FEBRUARY 14: Why There is a Saint Valentine’s Day,” pages 3, 4 and 6. The public-domain story describes an execution repeatedly. Proposed changes keep the fact that Valentine died but remove descriptions of how:

- Page 3, replace “If they didn’t like any one or imagined he might do them harm, they had him beheaded, and Bishop Valentine, as he was then called, was beheaded.” with “The rulers punished people they saw as a threat. Bishop Valentine was punished and later died.”
- Page 4, replace “His friends felt dreadfully that such a good man should be so cruelly murdered” with “His friends were very sad that such a good man had died”. Keep the rest of the sentence.
- Page 6, replace “and although he was so cruelly beheaded, still his deeds of charity and kindness will always be known” with “and his deeds of charity and kindness are still remembered”. Keep the rest of the sentence.

This is an adaptation, not a historical fact-check. Review surrounding text and quiz, preserve attribution, and record the alteration in the book credit if approved.

### P-002 — Fourth grade: Ali Baba

`en-4-ali-baba`, chapter 1, page 10. Replace the final sentence “The guide was so confounded that he knew not what answer to make, and when they returned he was at once beheaded for having failed.” with “The guide was so confounded that he knew not what answer to make, and when they returned, the Captain sent him away.” This changes the event to avoid the execution; it must be clearly identified as an adaptation. The chapter also includes other killings and an oil attack; review the entire scene before applying this isolated change or deciding whether the story should remain available.

The same chapter's generated quiz 2 includes the distractor “Two men must have known it; we have killed one, we must now find the other.” Propose replacing it with “Morgiana saw the chalk mark on the door.” Verify the chronology, correct answer, and full revised scene before applying. Quiz provenance and story provenance differ, and this option echoes the source text; it is included in the approval request.

## P-003 — Additional Ali Baba scenes, proposed for approval

**Not applied.** The user requested specific proposals for the additional scenes. These changes substantially adapt the story, replacing mutilation and fatal attacks with a burial, capture and escape. They must be applied together and reviewed against the full chapter and quiz for continuity.

In `en-4-ali-baba`, chapter 1:

1. **Page 6:** Replace “They cut Cassim’s body into four quarters, and nailed them up inside the cave, in order to frighten anyone who should venture in, and went away in search of more treasure.” with “They left Cassim in the cave and went away in search of more treasure.” The preceding page still says Cassim was killed, without describing an injury.
2. **Page 8:** Replace the first two sentences (the cobbler is blindfolded and asked to sew the body) with “The day after, Morgiana went to an old cobbler near the gates of the town. She asked him to help prepare burial clothes for Cassim and guided him to the house with his eyes covered.” Keep the burial and the remaining passage.
3. **Page 9:** Replace “I sewed a dead body together in a place where I had less light than I have now” with “I helped prepare burial clothes in a place where I had less light than I have now”; replace “the house where he stitched up the dead body” with “the house where he helped prepare the burial clothes.” This preserves how the thief finds the house.
4. **Page 14:** Replace the sentences beginning “She filled her oil pot” through “waited to see what would happen” with “She filled her oil pot, went back to the kitchen, and lit her lamp. Then she quietly called for help. Together, Morgiana and her neighbors secured the lids of the jars and sent for the town authorities.”
5. **Page 15:** Replace “On going to the first jar” through “became aware of the manner of their death” with “On going to the first jar, he found that the lids were secured and heard the neighbors and the town authorities approaching. He realized that his plot against Ali Baba and his household had been discovered.” Keep the Captain’s escape and Morgiana seeing it.
6. **Page 16:** Replace the first paragraph (from “At daybreak Ali Baba arose” through “while the mules were sold in the market by his slaves”) with “At daybreak Ali Baba saw the jars still in the yard. Morgiana explained that robbers had hidden inside them and that the supposed oil merchant was their Captain. She and the neighbors had secured the jars, and the town authorities had taken the robbers away. Grateful for her courage, Ali Baba gave Morgiana her freedom.” Keep the next paragraph, in which the Captain seeks revenge.
7. **Page 20:** Replace “Morgiana plunged the dagger into his heart” with “Morgiana caught his arm and called for help. Ali Baba and his son took away his dagger, and the town authorities led him away.” Change “what have you done to ruin us?” to “what is the matter?”; replace “Look at him! he is both the false oil merchant and the Captain of the Forty Thieves” with “He is both the false oil merchant and the Captain of the Forty Thieves.” Keep the rest of the ending.
8. **Page 21:** Replace “At the end of a year Ali Baba, hearing nothing of the two remaining robbers, judged they were dead” with “At the end of a year Ali Baba, having heard nothing more from the robbers”. This avoids the old count of dead robbers after the revised capture and the earlier approved change.

These revisions leave threats, a death without graphic detail, weapons, enslavement in the original narrative, and the arranged marriage in the chapter; those remain for grade-level and representation review. Before implementation, recheck generated quiz options and evidence against all changed passages and disclose the adaptation in the credit.
